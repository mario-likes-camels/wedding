Our Story

A collection of calculated risks, minor medical emergencies, and street furniture

2024
The "Forced" First Date

After connecting on Hinge, Natasha successfully convinced Patrick that a weekend date was already scheduled (definitely was not!) She then turned up fashionably late. But despite the trickery, a great stroll around the lake left them both eager for more. Natasha claimed she was busy and wouldn't be able to see Patrick for at least a week, only to invite him out again literally 24 hours later. They haven't stopped hanging out since, sharing massive, side-splitting laughs whenever they're around each other.

2024 (Part II)
The 4th Date Incident

Natasha proposed a low-stakes intro to Patrick's family: watching he and his sister play tennis, then have Patrick cook dinner for them all. A sweet idea, but Patrick's body had other ideas. After maybe a little too much exertion (trying to impress her?) Patrick spent the entire night violently vomiting, leaving Natasha to bond with his sister to a chaotic soundtrack. Undeterred, a few weeks later Natasha returned to the family home again and was won over by their charm. She then took on her greatest challenge: turning Patrick from an alien into a... slightly more normal human. Successfully initiating his social skills training has included teaching him how to drink coffee and properly brew tea (for her).

2025
The Melbourne Era

Up for an adventure (for 1-3 years), the couple packed up and moved to Melbourne. While Natasha absolutely tore it up at work, thriving and tackling all the hard stuff, Patrick embraced his destiny as the ideal house-husband. Together, they proudly furnished their apartment entirely with premium treasures found discarded on the street, building a beautiful home and beginning their transition toward something more meaningful.

2026
The Beach Proposal

On a rare sunny Melbourne day, Patrick hijacked a casual beach walk to pop the question. Under an ominous cliff overhang and just moments before they ran into a whole group of nude men letting it all hang out, Patrick pulled out a ring. Natasha, of course, said yes!

The Future
The Next Chapter

Natasha is set to ascend even further into her career, while Patrick continues to hone his skills in homemaking... and who knows, maybe even money-making too! In the meantime, the time has come to celebrate the union with our nearest and dearest family.
