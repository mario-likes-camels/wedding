Our Story

A collection of calculated risks, minor medical emergencies, and street furniture

2024
The "Forced" First Date

After connecting on Hinge, Natasha successfully convinced Patrick that a weekend date was already scheduled when it absolutely wasn't. She then turned up fashionably late. Despite the trickery, a great stroll around the lake left them both eager for more. Natasha claimed she was busy for a week, only to invite Patrick out again literally 24 hours later. They haven't stopped hanging out since, sharing massive, side-splitting laughs on every single date.

2024 (Slight Detour)
The 4th Date Incident & Humanization

Patrick played way too much tennis and spent the entire night violently vomiting, leaving Natasha to bond beautifully with his sister to a chaotic soundtrack. Surviving that, Natasha took on her greatest challenge: turning Patrick from an alien into a slightly more normal human. She successfully initiated his social skills training, which included teaching him how to drink coffee and properly brew tea.

2025
The Melbourne Era

We packed up and moved to Melbourne to build a life together. While Natasha absolutely tore shizz up at work, thriving and tackling all the hard stuff, Patrick embraced his destiny as the ideal house-husband. Together, they proudly furnished their apartment entirely with premium treasures found discarded on the street, building a beautiful home and beginning their transition toward marriage.

2026
The Beach Proposal

On a rare sunny Melbourne day, Patrick hijacked a casual beach walk to pop the question. Right in the middle of being lambasted for his lack of PhD progress—and amidst Natasha’s sheer despair that it would ever be finished—Patrick pulled out a ring. The magical moment happened under an ominous cliff overhang, just moments before they ran into a whole group of nude men letting it all hang out. She, of course, said yes!
